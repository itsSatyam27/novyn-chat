Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Novyn handles information when you use its website, messaging app, and calling features.

Information the service handles

Depending on the features you use, Novyn processes account details such as your username, email address, and profile; contacts and conversation content you submit; call records; and basic device, session, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.

How information is used and stored

Information is used to provide sign-in, messaging, calls, synchronization, notifications, account security, and support. Chats may be cached on your device and synchronized with Novyn's service backend. Firebase services support authentication and notifications. Uploaded media may be handled by the media hosting provider configured for the service.

Google Drive chat backups

If you choose Back up to Google Drive, Novyn requests access to its private app data folder in your Drive. The chat backup is encrypted with the password you choose before it is uploaded. It is stored for Novyn and does not appear in the normal Drive file list. Restore it in Novyn with the same Google account and backup password.

Keep your backup password safe. Novyn cannot decrypt the backup without it. The chat backup does not include your private end-to-end encryption identity key.

Service providers

Novyn relies on infrastructure providers for authentication, hosting, notifications, database, and media features. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services. Novyn does not use the promotional website to sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained while needed to provide the service, meet security and operational needs, or comply with applicable requirements. You can stop using the app and revoke Google Drive access in your Google Account settings.

Security and updates

Novyn uses security measures intended to protect information, but no internet service can promise absolute security. This policy may be updated as the service changes. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.

Contact

For account or privacy questions, email novynchat.app@gmail.com.
