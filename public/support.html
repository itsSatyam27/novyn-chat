Sign-in and connection

  • Check that your device is online, then close and reopen Novyn.
  • Update to the latest app version and try signing in again.
  • If sign-in still fails, email us with your device model, app version, and the exact error text.

Messages and calls

  • For messages that are not sending, check your connection and reopen the conversation.
  • For call issues, allow microphone and camera permissions in your device settings.
  • If a problem keeps happening, tell us what you tapped and what happened next.

Chat backup and restore

Open Novyn's backup and restore settings to create or restore a backup. Restore with the same Google account and the backup password you set when creating it.

Keep your backup password and encryption keys private. Never include them in a support email. If you no longer have the backup password, we cannot recover it for you.

Account and privacy

Review account, privacy, blocked contacts, and linked-device options in the app's Settings. For privacy questions or account access concerns, contact us using the email below. Do not send your password, verification codes, private keys, or message contents.

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for more information.

Contact support

Email us with a short description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it, your device model, and the app version. You can attach a screenshot after removing any private chat details.
